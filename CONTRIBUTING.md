# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve **Anti-Detect Browser Comparison** — an independent, MIT-licensed benchmark repo on GitHub.

## What belongs here

- Factual **anti-detect browser** / **fingerprint browser** comparisons  
- Pricing notes, coupon codes (`SAAS50`, `MIN50`), and migration checklists  
- Translations as Markdown in `docs/` (GitHub Search)  

## What we do not accept

- Impersonating official Multilogin, GoLogin, or other vendor sites  
- Scraped competitor branding, logos, or support contacts  
- Spam links unrelated to browser comparison  

## How to add a guide

1. Add `docs/your-topic.md` with a **keyword-rich filename** (e.g. `gologin-vs-multilogin.md`).  
2. Link it from [README.md](README.md) and [docs/README.md](docs/README.md).  
3. Keep tone educational; disclose affiliate links where used.  

## Citation

You may link to this repository or fork it under MIT. Please do not claim endorsement by any antidetect vendor.
