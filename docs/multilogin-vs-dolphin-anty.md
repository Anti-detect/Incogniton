## When Dolphin Anty is enough

- Script farms with disposable accounts  
- Lowest cost per profile  
- Platforms with loose fingerprint checks  

## When to choose Multilogin over Dolphin Anty

- Facebook / TikTok / Google ads at scale  
- E-commerce seller accounts you cannot afford to lose  
- In-house automation with Playwright
